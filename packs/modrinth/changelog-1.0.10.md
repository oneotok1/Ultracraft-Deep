- **No more ghost twins.** With a field of view above 90, some bosses (Gabriel, the Swordsmachine) were drawn twice side by side: one of ULTRAKILL's menus sat on the same layer as their models, so the HUD camera (fixed at 90) drew them a second time. The menu has its own layer now.
- **Gabriel stays out of the floor.** He's lifted out whenever he lands inside blocks (his teleports and dashes were made for ULTRAKILL's flat floors), so he no longer sinks into floors or shows through steps and walls. His entrance stands him on the floor too.
- **Out of bounds in boss arenas.** Fall off (or out of) a boss arena and you're put back at its way in, with no fall damage.
- **Gabriel's first meeting.** The first time you face the Judge of Hell he says "Your choice is made..." before "BEHOLD!"; in rematches he goes straight to "BEHOLD!". Boss Setup's Encounter setting now also applies to Fight Now and `/uc boss call`.

- **6-2's arena rebuilt** from the level itself: the long, high, bright gothic hall, its raised entrance, rows of skeletons on stakes down the sides (the middle clear), giant skulls on the walls, chandeliers, and the golden organ on its dais with candle piles either side.
